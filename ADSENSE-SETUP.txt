Google reviews the entire site, including content, navigation, policy compliance, ownership and other factors.

FINAL STEPS YOU MUST COMPLETE AFTER DEPLOYMENT
1. Deploy the site on your final domain and use that exact domain in AdSense.
2. Add the exact AdSense site/verification code Google gives you. Do not use a fake publisher ID.
3. Replace pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX in ads.txt with your real AdSense publisher ID and uncomment the google.com line.
4. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.example in sitemap.xml and robots.txt with your real absolute domain.
5. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.
6. Add canonical URLs using the final domain if/when you want explicit canonical tags.
7. In AdSense Privacy & messaging, configure the appropriate consent solution for your traffic. Google requires a certified CMP integrated with IAB TCF for personalized ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland.
8. Make sure the Privacy Policy accurately reflects every analytics, advertising, cookie and third-party service actually enabled.
9. Keep original, useful content and clear navigation. Do not rely on ads to make a thin site look complete.
10. Never click your own ads, ask others to click them, or place ads where accidental clicks are encouraged.

SECURITY NOTE
- The Supabase publishable/anon key is expected to be visible in a browser app; it is safe only when database/storage RLS and policies are correctly configured.
- Never put a Supabase service-role key, private API key, admin password or other server secret in HTML/JS.
- /admin.html is an entry point, not the security boundary. The database/backend authorization must remain enabled.
